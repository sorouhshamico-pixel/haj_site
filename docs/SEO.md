# SEO Strategy

## Site Architecture
- Homepage, about, services, hajj, umrah, blog, contact, FAQ, legal pages.
- Use short Arabic-friendly URLs and strong internal linking.

## Keyword Strategy
- Primary: حج من مصر, برامج الحج من مصر, برامج العمرة من مصر.
- Secondary: دليل الحج, مناسك العمرة, الأوراق المطلوبة للعمرة.

## Metadata Strategy
- Unique titles and descriptions per page.
- Canonical URLs and Open Graph tags provided by the app shell.

## Schema Strategy
- Organization, WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, Article, FAQPage where relevant.

## Internal Linking
- Link each hub to subtopic pages and blog articles.

## Image SEO
- Use descriptive file names and alt text.

## Technical SEO Checklist
- robots.txt, sitemap.xml, proper canonical tags, responsive layout, accessible markup.
